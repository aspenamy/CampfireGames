# Changelog

## 0.2.0

Solitaire is now a card table with five games to choose from.

- Klondike, Spider (1, 2 or 4 suits), Free Cell, Pyramid and Tri Peaks, each with its own record and how-to-play page.
- Klondike scoring: Standard, Vegas (with a running total across deals) or none.
- Winning ends with the cards bouncing off the table, like the old Windows game.
- A finished Spider run peels off the table with bells and a burst of sparks.
- Spider and Tri Peaks can use a wide table, which makes the window wider and the cards bigger, or a narrow one that fits the normal window.
- Four card backs to choose from.
- An option to play safe cards up to the foundations on their own.
- Hint button in Spider, and a Finish button in Klondike and Free Cell that plays the rest of the cards up once nothing can go wrong.
- Five new trophies for Spider, Free Cell, Pyramid and Tri Peaks.

## 0.1.0

First build, for World of Warcraft: Forever.

- Fifteen games: Sudoku, Gemcutter, Fireflies, Solitaire, Dig Site, Sliding Puzzle, Lanterns, Rune Lock, Pairs, Quill, Tapestry, Ricochet, Chimes, Pegs and Teahouse Tiles.
- Daily Sudoku and a daily Quill word, the same for everyone each day.
- Best scores, plays and wins for every game, trophies to earn, and a how-to-play page for each game.
- Small window that opens on the left, moves anywhere and resizes from the corner.
- Minimap button, key binding, `/campfire` and `/games`.
- Options in the window and in WoW's AddOns settings: sounds, firelight, string lights, a fireside crackle, hiding in combat, see-through when the mouse is away, raid marks for game pieces, and opening when you start fishing or take a flight path.
